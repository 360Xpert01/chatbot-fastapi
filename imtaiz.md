# Imtiaz Super Market (Imtiaz Mall) - Corporate Knowledge Base

## Overview
Imtiaz Super Market is Pakistan's largest retail chain, serving millions of customers across multiple cities including Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Gujranwala, Sialkot, Peshawar, and Bahawalpur. Imtiaz offers a massive range of grocery items, electronics, apparel, household goods, and cosmetics.

## Return, Exchange, and Refund Policy
* **Timeframe:** Items can be exchanged or returned within 3 days of purchase.
* **Condition:** Products must be unused, in original packaging, with all tags intact.
* **Requirement:** The original printed cash receipt (bill) is absolutely mandatory for any return or exchange processing.
* **Exceptions:** Perishable items (fresh meat, seafood, fruits, vegetables) and innerwear/apparel items cannot be returned or exchanged due to hygiene reasons.

---

## Imtiaz Loyalty & Reward Program (Imtiaz Cards)
* **Registration:** Customers can sign up for the Imtiaz Loyalty Program for free via the Imtiaz mobile app or at the Customer Relations desk at any mega branch.
* **Points Accumulation:** Earn points on every purchase by scanning the digital loyalty card at the cash counter before billing.
* **Redemption:** Points can be redeemed as direct discounts on subsequent grocery bills. 1 point equals a predetermined cash-back value set by management.

---

## E-Commerce & Online Delivery
* **Imtiaz Easy Shopping App:** Customers can order groceries online using the official Imtiaz mobile application available on Android Play Store and Apple App Store.
* **Delivery Areas:** Online delivery is available in major sectors of Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad/Rawalpindi.
* **Delivery Charges:** Flat delivery rates apply unless a promotional free-delivery tier threshold is met.
